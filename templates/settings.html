Organization and PKI policy

Used for CRL distribution URLs in newly issued certificates. Previously issued certificates keep their original URLs.

End-entity key downloads are audited. CA private keys can never be downloaded. Operators and auditors cannot download keys.

HTTPS service

127.0.0.1 permits local connections; 0.0.0.0 listens on all IPv4 interfaces. The packaged service automatically restarts after listener or certificate changes. Reconnect using the new address and port.

Upload both files to replace the initial HTTPS certificate. The server certificate must contain a Subject Alternative Name and match the private key. Leave both blank to retain the current certificate.
